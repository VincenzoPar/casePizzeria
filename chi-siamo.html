Our Story

A PROJECT DRIVEN
BY PASSION

At the heart of CASÉ is Crescenzo, a 27-year-old pizzaiolo from Caserta. For the past seven years, he has lived and worked in Vienna, where he has put his experience, technique, and passion for pizza-making at the service of the city, collaborating with several local venues and taking part in the opening of new Neapolitan pizzerias. After years of work, growth, and research, the desire to create a personal project was born: to open the first Caserta-style pizzeria in Vienna and bring a clear, authentic, and recognisable identity to the city. By his side is Viktoria, his life partner and also a partner in this project, whose international perspective and connection to Vienna help shape CASÉ into a welcoming and contemporary place.

HOW DO WE WORK

THREE THINGS
THAT MATTER

01

Dough

We work with a preferment, a choice that gives the dough greater flavour, a more balanced structure, and improved digestibility. The result is a pizza with a clear character: soft, aromatic, and full of depth.

02

Ingredients

We carefully select flours suited to medium and long fermentation, in line with a thoughtful and precise dough-making process. An important part of this journey is our collaboration with Molini Pizzuti, an Italian flour producer known for products designed for long-fermentation pizza doughs.

03

Traditions meet Research

Every pizza is the result of a meeting between tradition and research. Alongside the classics, we offer contemporary interpretations and original combinations, created to express a distinctive personal style.

Our Promise

NOT JUST
A PIZZA

CASÉ was created to bring a real piece of Caserta to Vienna — in flavour, in hospitality, and in the way we welcome people to the table. Every detail, from the dough to the dining room, is designed to communicate authenticity, quality, and a sense of home.

Walking into CASÉ means finding pizza made with intention, an honest atmosphere, and a project that puts people first.
