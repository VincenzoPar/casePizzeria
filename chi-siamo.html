Our Story

A PROJECT DRIVEN
BY PASSION

CASÉ is the shared project of Crescenzo and Viktoria, born from their love for great food, genuine hospitality, and the desire to create something of their own.

Originally from Caserta, Crescenzo has spent the past seven years living and working in Vienna, collaborating with local venues and helping open new Neapolitan pizzerias while refining his craft and staying true to the traditions of his hometown.

By his side is Viktoria, his life partner and partner in this project. Her international perspective and strong connection to Vienna have helped shape CASÉ into a welcoming and contemporary place. Together, they created Vienna's first Caserta-style pizzeria to share the flavours, techniques, and hospitality of Caserta.

HOW DO WE WORK

THREE THINGS
THAT MATTER

01

Dough

We work with a preferment, a choice that gives the dough greater flavour, a more balanced structure, and improved digestibility. The result is a pizza with a clear character: soft, aromatic, and full of depth.

02

Ingredients

We carefully select flours suited to medium and long fermentation, in line with a thoughtful and precise dough-making process. An important part of this journey is our collaboration with Molini Pizzuti, an Italian flour producer known for products designed for long-fermentation pizza doughs.

03

Traditions meet Research

Every pizza is the result of a meeting between tradition and research. Alongside the classics, we offer contemporary interpretations and original combinations, created to express a distinctive personal style.
